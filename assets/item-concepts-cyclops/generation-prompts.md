# Cyclops Item Concept Prompts

These assets were generated as concept-only pixel-art equipment sheets. Each prompt requested five standalone icons on a flat `#ff00ff` background so the backdrop could be converted to transparency.

## Shared Direction

- Crisp polished 32-bit pixel art
- Strong inventory-scale silhouettes
- Consistent perspective and pixel density
- Cyclops Button palette: black, charcoal, gold, red visor glass, green portal energy, steel, and white-gold
- Simple-to-ornate rarity progression from left to right
- No text, logos, watermarks, characters wearing the equipment, bunny imagery, carrots, clovers, or Pokemon imagery

## Lenses

Five distinct lens items: cracked red visor lens, gold one-eye targeting monocle, green portal prism lens, black obsidian scope with one glowing red eye, and ancient white-gold all-seeing lens relic.

## Boots

Five distinct pairs: worn arcade runner boots with red visor stripes, black-and-gold bay technician boots, green portal-step greaves, heavy silver metal-race combat boots, and ancient white-gold tournament champion boots with a single-eye gem.

## Cores

Five distinct mechanical power cores: salvaged battery core with a red visor light, black-and-gold arcade power cell, green portal reactor core, dense stone-and-metal arena heart, and ancient white-gold singularity core with one vertical aperture.

## Charms

Five distinct pendants: simple iron one-eye token, gold red-visor pendant, green portal shard charm, black tournament Mark medallion with a single red eye, and ancient white-gold orbital charm surrounding one vertical eye gem.

## Relics

Five distinct relics: scratched arcade token with a red visor slit, black-and-gold miniature button shrine, floating green portal fragment in a metal frame, stone tournament Mark tablet with one red illuminated groove, and ancient white-gold crown artifact built around one vertical visor aperture.
